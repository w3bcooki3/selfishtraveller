      Seven years, one question: what's over the next ridge?

      I grew up in Manali, at the edge of the Pir Panjal, which meant the mountains were never a destination — they were just where the horizon happened to sit. Ten years ago I started carrying a camera on treks that used to be just for me. It changed how I walked. You notice more when you're looking for a shot.

      Since then I've spent close to seven years on trails across Nepal, Patagonia, the Alps, and home in the Himalayas — filming, writing, and mostly just paying attention. I'm not chasing summits for the sake of a list. I'm interested in what a place looks like on day nine, when the novelty's worn off and something more honest starts to show.
